ABOUT US — RUDRAM PLATFORM

RUDRAM is an advanced AI-powered disaster intelligence and emergency response platform engineered for multi-hazard risk prediction, real-time meteorological tracking, automated GIS mapping, and safe shelter navigation.

Purpose & Mission:
To empower disaster management authorities, emergency first responders, and citizens with real-time risk telemetry, calibrated XGBoost prediction models, live shelter routing, and situation briefing reports to protect human life and infrastructure.
PLATFORM POLICIES & RESPONSIBILITIES
1. Data Usage & Telemetry
RUDRAM aggregates live weather data from Open-Meteo, satellite observations from NASA Earthdata, and geographical boundaries from official GIS feeds for non-commercial disaster mitigation.
2. User Privacy
Browser GPS location data accessed for real-time risk assessment is processed locally on-device and is never shared, sold, or retained for tracking purposes.
3. Emergency Information Disclaimer
RUDRAM ML models provide probabilistic risk assessments. During active emergency evacuations, always prioritize official directives from OSDMA, Special Relief Commissioner, and local authorities.
4. User Responsibilities
Citizens and emergency responders are expected to report emergency incidents accurately and refrain from transmitting false SOS alerts or misleading hazard reports.
